Why not WhatsApp?

A group chat only shows history from the moment you join it. Join late, change sections, or transfer in as a lateral entry student, and everything shared before you arrived is gone for good.

How EduVault fixes it

Material is stored against the teacher's profile in a database, not inside a disappearing chat thread — so a lookup by Employee ID always returns the full history, no matter when your account was created.
